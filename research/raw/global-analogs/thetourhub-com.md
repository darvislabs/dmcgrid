The process

## How it works

From a pasted brief to a shared quote in four steps. Atlas builds; you decide; TourHub runs the rest.

01

### Paste or speak the brief

An email, a document, a package link. Atlas does not ask questions; it builds the best draft it can from what you gave it.

02

### Review the canvas

Legs, rooms, golf, transfers, activities, each one resolved to a supplier. Adjust anything on the leg cards.

03

### Generate and share

Three quotes in one click. Preview the presentation, share the link, watch the readiness checklist fill in.

04

### TourHub runs the rest

Supplier emails, invoices, payments, vouchers, and the ledger. Amend before or after settlement without breaking anything.
