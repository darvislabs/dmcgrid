## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is SWT CLUB and who can book with it?

SWT CLUB is an IATA-accredited B2B wholesale travel company — a Global Online DMC built only for travel agents, never for direct consumers. We were founded in 2007, and in those 19 years we've handled 200,000+ trips for 5,000+ travel agents across India. Hotels, flights, holiday packages, visas, transfers, MICE — booked globally across 195+ countries, at net rates. Your client never sees us. That's the point — you own the customer, we power the booking.

### How do I register as a travel agent partner with SWT CLUB?

Submit your agency details — GST, IATA/TAAI/TAFI membership if you have one, basic KYC — through our partner registration page, and you're live within 24–48 hours. From day one you get a dedicated relationship manager (a named person who picks up your call, not a ticket number), access to wholesale net rates, and Partner Desk support. Zero franchise fee. Zero lock-in. Zero minimum billing. You joined this industry to run your own business — we keep it that way.

### What can travel agents book through SWT CLUB — and why are the rates better?

Everything a full-stack DMC handles: FIT holidays, group departures, MICE movements, IATA consolidator air fares, hotels, visas, travel insurance, and on-ground services in 195+ countries. We contract directly and buy wholesale — you get the net rate and set your own markup, your margin stays yours. 5,000+ agents and 200,000+ trips since 2007 means the rates you see have been battle-tested by two decades of real bookings, not burned on discounts that vanish next quarter.

### Why do travel agents choose SWT CLUB over other B2B portals?

Simple test: when your client wants Georgia, Peru, Kenya — or a 200-pax MICE group to Istanbul — does your portal go quiet? Most B2B platforms run deep in 15-18 popular destinations and empty everywhere else. SWT CLUB books globally across 195+ countries, because we were built as a wholesale DMC, not a booking app with packages bolted on. And the record backs it: founded 2007, 200,000+ trips, 5,000+ agent partners, IATA accreditation, Ministry of Tourism recognition, and four nominations for India's Best B2B Travel Provider — World Travel Tech Awards (2021, 2022, 2025) and the World Travel Awards 2026. Apps are great for the easy bookings. We're who you call for the rest.
