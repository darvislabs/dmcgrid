 Travel Agency Software Features

## Complete Travel Agency CRM &
Itinerary Builder Software

From professional itinerary building and PDF export to GST invoicing, booking vouchers, lead management, and team control — ChinarCRM is India's complete travel agency management software.

### Itinerary Builder Software

Build professional day-by-day itineraries for Kashmir, Goa, Rajasthan or any destination. Add hotels, cabs, activities, and pricing. Export as premium PDF or share via link instantly.

### PDF Itinerary Generator

Generate branded PDF itineraries in Classic, Luxury, Modern, and Nature themes. Include your logo, UPI QR code, and bank details. Looks like a ₹10,000 brochure — built in minutes.

### Travel CRM & Lead Management

Track every enquiry through your pipeline. Add follow-ups, notes, status updates. Convert leads to confirmed bookings without losing a single customer.

### Booking Vouchers

Create hotel vouchers, cab vouchers, flight vouchers, and activity confirmations. Track vendor payments and share confirmations professionally.

### GST Invoicing for Travel Agents

Generate GST-ready invoices and quotations designed for Indian travel businesses. Compliant, professional, and instant — no accounting software needed.

### Travel Manifest & Pax Tracking

Manage passenger lists, arrival/departure schedules, and ground transport for every trip. Your drivers and hotels always have the right information.

### Commission Tracking

Track agent commissions automatically. Set rules per agent, approve payouts, and maintain full audit trails for every confirmed booking.

### Vendor & Hotel Management

Store all your hotels, transport vendors, guides, and partners. Access rates, contacts, and booking history without leaving the platform.

### Team & Role Management

Granular access control for owners, managers, and agents. Everyone sees exactly what they need — secure, organised, and fully audited.
