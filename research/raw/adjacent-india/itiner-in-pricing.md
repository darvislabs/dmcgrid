## FAQ

### Is there a free plan?

Yes. You can start on the Free plan with no credit card required.

### What currency is pricing in?

All plan prices are listed in Indian rupees (INR), billed monthly.

### What is founding member pricing?

Early adopters get Flex and Growth at up to half the list price. Upgrade now and your rate is locked in for a year.

### How do I upgrade?

Pick the plan you want and send us a note from the contact page. We move your workspace onto it ourselves, usually the same day, and your founding-member rate is locked for a year from that date. Because a person handles every upgrade rather than a checkout, you can raise anything specific to your agency at the same time — an extra seat, a different billing cycle, a question about white-labelling — and have it settled before you pay rather than after.

### Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade as your agency grows.

### Do the trip and AI limits reset?

Yes. Trip and AI generation allowances reset at the start of every calendar month. Your client list is cumulative and is never reset.

### What does white-label mean?

On Growth and above, the "Powered by Itiner" line disappears from client portals, PDF exports, and the itinerary email. Flex keeps the "Curated by [Agency] · Powered by Itiner" footer.

### What does the Custom plan include?

Custom is for large travel companies with big agent teams: unlimited usage, multi-agent workflows, and custom feature requests. Talk to the founder and we will scope it with you.
