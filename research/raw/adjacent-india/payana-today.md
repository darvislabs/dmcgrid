 Questions

## Answers, no *fine print.*

How long does onboarding take?
Most agencies are live in a week. We help you migrate your customer list, upload your logo and brand colour, connect your WhatsApp Business number, and configure your PDF and email templates. Growth and White-label plans include guided onboarding calls.

Do I keep my WhatsApp number?
Yes. Payana uses the official WhatsApp Business API, so your existing number is upgraded — you don't get a new one. Customers see the same number, with a verified green tick. No data is lost during migration.

What if my traveler doesn't install the app?
They don't have to. Every itinerary you generate is also a branded web page, a WhatsApp message, and a PDF. The app is the upgraded experience for travelers who want it. Document delivery doesn't depend on it.

How is GST handled on quotes & invoices?
Payana applies 5% GST on tour packages by default and switches to 18% for travel-arrangement-only services. TCS is automatically computed above the ₹7 L threshold for overseas trips. Invoices include your GSTIN, HSN code, and place of supply — ready for filing.

Can I export my data if I leave?
Always. Export customers, trips, leads, and invoices as CSV at any time. We don't hold your data hostage. There's a full-account ZIP download in Settings if you want everything in one go.

Is there a setup or migration fee?
No. Starter and Growth plans are pure subscription. White-label customers may opt for paid onboarding if they want bespoke integrations, a custom domain set up by us, or data migration from legacy CRMs — that's quoted separately.
