## What is a B2B travel portal?

A **B2B travel portal** is an online booking platform restricted to travel trade companies rather than the public. Registered agents log in and see **net rates**, wholesale prices excluding retail margin, for hotels, transfers, tours and tickets, then add their own markup when selling to the traveller. Unlike a consumer OTA, a B2B portal verifies each account against a real trading business, shows trade-only pricing, and issues a single consolidated invoice rather than taking card payment from the end traveller.

The practical benefit is inventory access without contracts. Rather than negotiating individually with hotels, transfer operators and ticket suppliers in every destination, an agent books all of it from one login, against one credit line, with one point of support when something goes wrong at 2am in another time zone.

## Net rates: how B2B pricing differs from retail

A **net rate** is the price the trade pays before any agent margin is added. It is not a discount on a public price. It is a different price list, and it is confidential. Three practical consequences:

- **You set the selling price.** Your margin is whatever you add on top of net, so it is yours to control rather than a fixed commission percentage.
- **Rates are not publishable.** Trade rates normally may not be displayed publicly or undercut a supplier’s own public pricing. That is a standard rate-parity condition.
- **Terms differ from retail.** Cancellation deadlines, amendment rules and payment terms are set by the contract, not by consumer booking-site policy.
For agents, DMC Quote is free to use. There is no subscription or per-booking platform fee. Suppliers wanting to list inventory pay a one-time USD 50 setup fee and then commission only.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a B2B travel portal?

A B2B travel portal is an online booking platform open only to verified travel businesses. Registered agents see net wholesale rates for hotels, transfers, tours and tickets, then add their own margin when selling to travellers. Travel DMC Group’s portal is DMC Quote at dmcquote.com.

### How much does it cost a travel agent to join?

Nothing. Registration and use of the portal are free for travel agents. There is no subscription and no per-booking platform fee. Suppliers who want to list their own inventory pay a one-time USD 50 setup fee and commission thereafter.

### How long does account verification take?

Usually about 24 hours on a working day. You submit company details and travel-agency licence information at registration, and login credentials follow once those are checked.

### What is a net rate in travel?

A net rate is the wholesale price a travel agent pays before adding their own selling margin. It is a separate confidential price list rather than a discount off a public price, and it normally may not be displayed publicly or used to undercut the supplier’s own advertised pricing.

### Can individual travellers book on the B2B portal?

No. The portal is restricted to verified travel trade companies: agencies, tour operators, consolidators, corporate travel managers and other DMCs. Individual travellers should contact us and we will refer them to an agent.

### What is the difference between a DMC and a B2B travel portal?

A DMC is the company operating services on the ground in a destination: contracting hotels, running transfers, employing guides. A B2B travel portal is the booking technology agents use to buy those services. Travel DMC Group is both: the DMC in our own markets, and the portal through which agents book.

### Do you offer credit terms to agents?

Credit terms are arranged case by case with registered agencies depending on trading history and volume. New accounts normally start on prepayment; contact our sales team to discuss a credit facility.
