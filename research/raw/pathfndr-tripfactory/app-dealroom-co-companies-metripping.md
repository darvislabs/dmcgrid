Pathfndr, which appears to have evolved from the brand MeTripping, operates as a B2B travel technology provider based in Gurugram, India. The company was founded in 2015 by Varun Gupta. Gupta's background includes a decade of experience in the travel and e-commerce sectors, with previous roles at prominent companies like Yahoo, Expedia, and HolidayIQ. This experience in building and scaling online travel products directly informs Pathfndr's strategic direction.

The firm specializes in offering an AI-powered travel operating system designed for travel advisors, agents, and other businesses within the travel ecosystem. Its core product is a platform that leverages artificial intelligence to help travel professionals create highly customized and complex travel itineraries in a fraction of the time it would take manually. This service aims to empower traditional travel intermediaries to compete with larger online travel agencies (OTAs) by enhancing their efficiency and service capabilities. The platform functions on a plug-and-play basis, allowing for seamless integration into a client's existing website. Pathfndr's business model is primarily commission-based, earning revenue from the bookings made through its platform.

A significant milestone for the company was securing pre-seed funding, with notable investment from Haresh Chawla, a partner at True North. This capital injection was aimed at expanding the company's global footprint and further developing its technology. The platform provides a comprehensive suite of tools for travel businesses, enabling them to build, manage, and distribute travel products. By offering an end-to-end fulfillment infrastructure, Pathfndr assists businesses in creating a diverse range of travel experiences for their customers.
