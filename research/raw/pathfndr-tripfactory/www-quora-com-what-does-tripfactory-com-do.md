TripFactory.com is an online travel technology and services company that helps travel sellers create, manage and distribute packaged holidays and dynamic itineraries. Its platform and services target tour operators, travel agencies, online travel agencies (OTAs), destination management companies (DMCs) and travel product suppliers who need automated packaging, distribution and retail tools.

Core offerings and capabilities

- Dynamic packaging engine

- Assemble flight + hotel + transfers + activity combinations in real time.
- Price markups, commissions, currency conversion and fare rules applied automatically.
- Support for ancillaries (seat selection, baggage) and room/occupancy rules.
- Product catalogue & supplier connectivity

- Integrates supplier content (GDS, bedbanks, hotel APIs, activity suppliers) into a unified catalogue.
- Maps rates, inventories and booking rules; normalizes disparate data formats.
- B2B/B2C distribution

- White-label booking storefronts for agencies and tour operators.
- API/affiliate feeds for OTAs, travel agents and partners to consume packaged products.
- Wholesaler and reseller management with tiered pricing and commission controls.
- Booking & order management

- End-to-end booking flow: search, pricing, booking, PNR/ticket issuance and voucher generation.
- Booking lifecycle tools: cancellations, amendments, refunds, supplier confirmations.
- Consolidated PNR and itinerary documents for multi-component bookings.
- Pricing, merchandising & product rules

- Rule-based pricing engine supporting promotions, seasonal rates and customizable markup logic.
- Product bundling and upsell/cross-sell capabilities at search and checkout.
- Back-office operations

- Reporting, reconciliation, audit trails and financial settlement modules.
- Inventory and supplier management, contract and rate maintenance.
- Localization & compliance

- Multi-currency, multi-language storefront support and local tax/GST handling.
- Payment gateway integrations and localized payment flows.
Who uses it and why

- Small-to-mid tour operators and travel agents use TripFactory to automate packaging and launch online retail channels without building the technology stack in-house.
- OTAs and resellers use its APIs/feeds to expand product breadth quickly.
- DMCs and wholesalers use the platform to distribute products to trade partners while controlling pricing and availability.
Typical benefits

- Faster time-to-market for packaged holiday products.
- Reduced manual booking work and fewer booking errors.
- Centralized control over pricing, rules and distribution.
- Ability to offer dynamic, real-time packaging to customers and partners.
Notes on market position and availability

- TripFactory operates as a technology provider rather than a consumer-facing meta-search engine; services vary by region and depending on partner integrations.
- Product features and integrations evolve—information reflects capabilities available up to May 2024.
