## Mobile Experience: Full CRM vs. Client App Only

Pixie Dust CRM ships the full agent CRM on your phone. Dashboard, bookings, clients, itineraries, financials, messaging, tasks, reports — everything you can do on the web, you can do from your iPhone or Android. It's not a companion app or a notification viewer. It's the entire CRM.

TravelJoy has “Itineraries by TravelJoy” on iOS and Android — but that's a **client-facing** app for viewing trip details, not an agent tool. Agents work exclusively from desktop or web. There is no TravelJoy agent mobile app.

This matters because travel agents don't sit at desks all day. You're at soccer practice, in the carpool line, at the grocery store, or on vacation yourself. When a client texts “what time is our dining reservation?” you need the answer in your pocket — not on a laptop you left at home.

## Disney & Cruise Specialization

Pixie Dust CRM was built from day one for agents who sell Disney World, Disneyland, Disney Cruise Line, Universal Orlando, and Royal Caribbean. That means Disney-specific booking fields (resort, room type, ticket tier, dining plan, MagicBand status), a built-in [dining reservation tracker](/features/booking-management), trip countdowns in the client app, and UI that speaks your language.

TravelJoy is a generalist CRM. It works for all travel types — luxury, adventure, corporate, destination weddings, Disney — but it doesn't specialize in any of them. A Disney World booking is treated the same as a safari or a corporate retreat. You won't find dining plan fields, Lightning Lane booking date calculators, or park reservation tracking built in. You'd need to rely on custom fields and manual workarounds.

If you're a generalist agent who sells everything, TravelJoy 's flexibility is a strength. If 70%+ of your bookings are Disney, Universal, or cruise, you're using a generic tool for a specialized job. For a deeper look, read our [full CRM comparison guide](/blog/best-crm-disney-travel-agents).
