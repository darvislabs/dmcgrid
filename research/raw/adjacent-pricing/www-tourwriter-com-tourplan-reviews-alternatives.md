## Itinerary building, accounting and suppliers

### Building a multi-day trip

In **Tourplan** you enter service codes and dates, and the system stacks them into a list you export as a document. It handles enormous complexity and strong operations reporting, but the output is rarely client-ready without a third-party design tool.

In **[Tourwriter](/product/)** you drag components from your library, pre-loaded images and descriptions load instantly, margins update live, and you send a mobile web link the client opens on their phone.

### Accounting

**Tourplan** keeps a full general ledger inside the software, which suits operators processing very high invoice volumes.

**Tourwriter** takes a best-in-breed approach and is the first travel platform to reach [Certified Xero Integration](/xero/) status: a 2-way sync that pushes invoices and supplier bills and marks bookings paid when the bank reconciles. QuickBooks export is also supported.

### Suppliers and product data

Both platforms model suppliers, products and rates in depth. **Tourplan** enforces hard inventory rules and blocks bookings that break an allotment.

**Tourwriter** stores option codes, separates guide notes from client notes, and sends one grouped booking email per supplier instead of many.

COMMON QUESTIONS

## Frequently asked questions

Is Tourplan an ERP system?
Yes. Tourplan is a travel-specific ERP covering inventory, reservations, operations and a native general ledger. Tourwriter is not an ERP. It focuses on itinerary design, quoting and booking, and connects to cloud accounting such as Xero.

How much does Tourplan cost?
Tourplan does not publish pricing. Operators who use it report roughly USD $900 to $1,200 per month for 3 to 4 users, with groups and enterprise setups higher, plus implementation. Tourwriter starts at USD $99 per user per month (Starter) and $149 (Pro), with setup from $1,199. Figures are indicative and accurate as of August 2026.

Can you switch from Tourplan to Tourwriter?
Yes, and it is the most common migration path. Tourwriter has moved DMCs off Tourplan in under four months, faster than a typical Tourplan implementation. Suppliers, contacts and product data import. Budget time for data cleanup and export your historical records first.

Does Tourwriter integrate with Xero?
Yes. Tourwriter is the first travel platform to achieve Certified Xero Integration status, with a 2-way sync that pushes invoices and supplier bills and marks a booking paid when the bank reconciles. QuickBooks export is also supported.

Can Tourplan create visual itineraries?
Tourplan produces functional PDF and Word documents. For high-end visuals, many Tourplan users add third-party tools such as Wetu or Travefy. Tourwriter builds visual, mobile-responsive itineraries inside the platform.

Which system is better for small tour operators?
Tourwriter generally suits small to mid-size DMCs of 1 to 50 staff, with lower cost, faster setup and a gentler learning curve. Tourplan earns its keep at group, series and wholesale scale.

Do I need technical skills to use Tourwriter or Tourplan?
Tourwriter needs minimal technical skill and most users are productive within 2 to 4 weeks. Tourplan has a steeper learning curve, often months, and teams frequently rely on one in-house expert to keep it running.

What happens to my data if I switch systems?
Both platforms support migration. Export your historical data first. Tourplan to Tourwriter is a common path. Budget 30 to 90 days for data cleanup.
